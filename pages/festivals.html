OUR FESTIVALS

Discover the most popular festivals in the Philippines.

SINULOG FESTIVAL

📍 Cebu City, Cebu

📅 3rd Sunday of January

A grand celebration in honor of the Santo Niño. It features street dancing, parades, and fluvial processions.

ATI-ATIHAN FESTIVAL

📍 Kalibo, Aklan

📅 3rd Sunday of January

Known as the Mother of All Philippine Festivals. People paint their faces and dance to lively drums.

DINAGYANG FESTIVAL

📍 Iloilo City, Iloilo

📅 4th Sunday of January

A thanksgiving festival for a bountiful harvest. It showcases vibrant street dancing competitions.

PANAGBENGA FESTIVAL

📍 Baguio City, Benguet

📅 February

Also known as the Baguio Flower Festival. It features beautiful floats, flowers, and street dancing.

KADAYAWAN FESTIVAL

📍 Davao City, Davao del Sur

📅 August

A celebration of life, culture, and bountiful harvest.
